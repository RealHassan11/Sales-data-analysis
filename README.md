# Sales-data-analysis
Sales Data Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyses sales data to understand business performance, identify top-performing products and regions, and uncover insights that can support better business decisions.

Objectives

* Analyse overall sales performance.
* Identify top-performing products.
* Compare sales across regions.
* Present key findings through an Excel dashboard.

Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* PivotTables and charts
* Data visualisation and dashboard reporting

Key Findings

* Top-performing product: Smartphones recorded the highest product sales value, totalling ₦82,287,020.
* Top-performing region: Lagos achieved the highest regional sales value, totalling ₦85,043,210.
* Sales trend: Sales fluctuated across the periods analysed, indicating variations in performance over time.

Business Insights and Recommendations

1. Product Performance

Smartphones were the leading product by sales value. The business should monitor smartphone demand, maintain appropriate inventory levels, and examine model-level performance to identify opportunities for growth.

2. Regional Performance

Lagos was the strongest-performing region by sales value. The business should investigate the factors contributing to this performance and assess opportunities to apply effective sales strategies in other regions.

3. Sales Trend Analysis

Sales fluctuated over the periods analysed. Further investigation into seasonal patterns, promotions, inventory availability, and customer demand could help explain these variations and support better sales planning.

Conclusion

This analysis demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to organise sales data, visualise performance, and identify commercially relevant patterns. The findings highlight smartphones and Lagos as the leading product and region by sales value, while the fluctuating sales trend presents an opportunity for further investigation and improved planning.
